# 0038: Mail, after Spark, on the accounts

- Status: accepted (the engine and the app; tested against a real inbox once Google is
  signed in with mail)
- Date: 2026-10-19

## Context

The owner wants an email client on the phone like Spark: a smart inbox, a swipe to archive
and one to snooze. The internet accounts (ADR 0032) already sign in to Google (OAuth) and
iCloud (an app-specific password) for contacts and calendars.

## Decision

**Our own small client: an engine in the agent package (`mobarchy_mcp.mail`, the
`mobarchy-mail` command, IMAP and SMTP from Python's standard library) and an app in the
shell (`MailApp`, through `MailData`).**

- **Accounts.** Google with OAuth (XOAUTH2 for IMAP and SMTP; the sign-in asks for
  `https://mail.google.com/` too, so an account signed in before needs to sign in again),
  iCloud with its app-specific password. The servers are known by the account's type.
- **A local copy.** The inbox's last 45 days (headers and the first 64 KB, for a snippet)
  in SQLite (`~/.cache/mobarchy/mail/mail.db`); a message is fetched whole when read.
- **The smart inbox.** People, notifications and newsletters, from the headers alone
  (`List-Unsubscribe`/`List-Id`/bulk: newsletters; no-reply senders, `Auto-Submitted`:
  notifications; the rest: people). The app opens on People.
- **Swipes.** Right: archive (into the account's `\Archive`, or Gmail's All Mail). Left:
  snooze (later today, this evening, tomorrow, this weekend, next week): the message goes
  into a `Snoozed` folder on the server and the phone keeps when it comes back; at that time
  it is moved back into the inbox, unread, and listed at the top.
- **Following.** `mobarchy-mail watch` (the user service `mobarchy-mail`) keeps an IMAP
  IDLE per account, syncs when the server says something changed, and shows a notification
  for new mail from people; each minute it brings back what is due.
- **Reading and writing.** The text (HTML made readable), attachments saved to Downloads;
  reply (in the thread), forward, archive, snooze, delete; a new message from any account.
  Gmail keeps what is sent; other servers get a copy in Sent.
- **Frigg.** `mail_list`, `mail_read`, `mail_archive`, `mail_snooze`, and `mail_send`
  (which asks the owner first).

## Not done (yet)

- Other IMAP servers (by address), folders beyond the inbox, search on the server.
- HTML shown as HTML (it is made into text), images in mail.
- Snoozed messages come back when the phone is on; another client sees them in Snoozed.
- Email addresses in Contacts (to pick a recipient).
